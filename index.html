Demographic Framework

The Two Phases of Census 2027

The Census of India is executed in two distinct, sequential phases to ensure exhaustive national coverage.

PHASE 01 31 Questions

Houselisting & Housing Census

Creates a comprehensive national inventory of all physical structures, residential premises, household amenities, and assets.

Building materials: Wall, roof & floor composition
Drinking water source & location (within or outside premises)
Lighting source, latrine access, drainage & cooking fuel
Assets: Television, Internet, computer, two-wheeler/four-wheeler
PHASE 02 29 Questions

Population Enumeration

Headcount and individual socio-demographic survey of every resident living in India during the enumeration reference period.

Name, relationship to head, gender, date of birth & age
Mother tongue, other languages known & highest educational attainment
Economic activity, occupation, industry & category of worker
Place of birth, place of last residence & duration of migration

Statutory Shield

Your Privacy & Legal Protection

Individual Census schedules enjoy absolute confidentiality under Section 15 of the Census Act, 1948.

Court Inadmissibility

Under Section 15 of the Census Act 1948, your individual answers cannot be accessed by police, tax authorities, or produced as evidence in any judicial court.
